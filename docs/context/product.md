# Product

Event Horizon is Francisco Soares’s portfolio for hiring teams seeking backend and applied AI engineering skills. English is the initial language, with a Portuguese switch.

The experience uses a dark space theme and an animated black hole opening while keeping career content readable. Sections cover selected projects, experience, skills, education, and contact. Projects appear in this order: Condoasa, Enterprise RAG, Acolher, Support Copilot. Expandable engineering notes explain technical decisions.

The supplied résumé is the factual source. Numerical claims are presented as reported project results. LinkedIn could not be fetched during implementation. Do not invent project links, screenshots, testimonials, or benchmarks.

Delivery is local only. No deployment, contact form, authentication, or backend is included. Contact uses email reveal, GitHub, and LinkedIn. The downloadable résumé is Portuguese even when the page is English.
