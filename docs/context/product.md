# Product

Event Horizon is Francisco Soares’s portfolio for hiring teams seeking backend and applied AI engineering skills. English is the default, with Portuguese available through the language switch. Saved language preferences override the default when local storage is available.

## Experience

The design follows the supplied “Engineering Beyond the Ordinary” mockup, with a dark space backdrop, copper headline, compact navigation, and glass project cards. Text and controls are HTML; the decorative black hole animates a background image through WebGL. A bilingual pause/resume control and static reduced-motion fallback keep the content usable without animation.

Navigation covers work, expertise, about, and contact. Condoasa, Enterprise RAG, and Acolher are initially visible. Each card opens its description, reported metrics, and engineering notes using native details/summary. “View all projects” reveals Support Copilot. Career content covers experience, skills, and education.

Contact uses email reveal, GitHub, and LinkedIn. The downloadable résumé is Portuguese in both languages. Language switching rebuilds the page, restores focus to the language button, and recreates the scene. Project expansion, revealed email, and manual animation pause state reset on that rebuild.

## Content and delivery

The supplied résumé is the factual source. Numerical claims are reported project results with their evaluation context. Direct project destinations and screenshots have not been supplied or verified; do not invent them. LinkedIn was not independently fetched during initial implementation.

The application is entirely static, with no contact form, authentication, database, or backend. A GitHub Pages build/deploy workflow exists, and an earlier deployment's compiled asset delivery was verified. The mockup redesign and enhanced animation were implemented and checked locally; those tasks did not publish a deployment. See [test evidence](../execution/test_evidence.md) for the distinction between local browser checks and historical deployment checks.
